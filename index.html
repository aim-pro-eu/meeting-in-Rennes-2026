How to get to Rennes?

Rennes is located in western France, in Brittany.

🚄

By train

When coming from neighbouring countries by train:

  • Paris has several railway stations served by high-speed trains. You will often need to change station using the subway.
  • The station for departures to Rennes is Paris Montparnasse.
✈️

By plane

When coming from other countries, fly into one of the Paris airports — Roissy–Charles de Gaulle or Orly — then take the train from Montparnasse.

See the plane travel plan →

Plane Travel Plan

Roissy–Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Orly (ORY) are the major airports in Paris. From either one, head to Gare Montparnasse to catch your train to Rennes.

ORY

From Orly airport

Métro 14 to Châtelet, then Métro 4 to Montparnasse – Bienvenüe

⏱️ ~40 min 🗺️ Itinerary
CDG

From Roissy–Charles de Gaulle airport

RER B to Châtelet – Les Halles, then Métro 4 to Montparnasse – Bienvenüe

⏱️ ~1 h 🗺️ Itinerary
